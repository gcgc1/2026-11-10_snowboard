# Snowboarding Fundamentals Resources

Curated, high-trust sources. Lessons draw knowledge from here rather than from parametric memory. Communities (wisdom) are listed separately.

## Knowledge

### Authoritative frameworks

- [PSIA-AASI: The Six Snowboarding Fundamentals (Performance Guide, PDF)](https://thesnowpros.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/SB_snowboard_performance_PG-nov2022.pdf)
  The official US instructor body's training standard (F1–F6). Use for: the technical model of *any* turn; the spine of Lessons 1–5.
- [CASI QuickRide System — Instructor's Reference Guide (PDF)](https://members.casi-acms.com/documents/Ref_Guide.pdf)
  Canada's beginner teaching methodology. Use for: how to sequence a beginner progression ("one thing at a time", building blocks, immediate feedback).
- [PSIA-AASI "Beginner's Guide to Snowboarding" — video series (Chris Rogers)](https://thesnowpros.org/take-a-lesson/beginners-guide-to-snowboarding)
  What the official body shows a first-day rider. Use for: gear fit, skating/gliding/standing up, stopping, slipping, and the chairlift (Lesson 8).
  - [Part 1 — gear, stance, carrying](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lF2lf5RGCOw)
  - [Part 2 — skate, glide, get up, climb](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9YGruvaETK4)
  - [Part 3 — stop, turn (garlands → C-turns), chairlift](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eIcLMojBopA) — matches Lesson 1.

### Beginner instruction & drills

- [Malcolm Moore (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@malcolmmoore)
  BASI Level 4 instructor; the clearest technique explanations available. Use for: knee steering, edge-change drills, fixing counter-rotation.
  - ["Snowboard Turn Exercises (Ranked Worst to Best)"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=60HUUK-zv-c) — dissects bad habits (counter-rotation) and good ones (knee steering, ski-pole drill).
  - ["Knee Steering // Torsional Twist Explained"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0dTYSztKisc) — the definitive explanation of the movement that replaces counter-rotation (Lesson 5).
- [Snowboard Pro Camp (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@SnowboardProCamp)
  Ex-Whistler instructor; broad, beginner-friendly. Use for: alternative explanations of anything that didn't land from another source.
- [Tommie Bennett (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@tommiebennett)
  Strong on posture and body mechanics. Use for: stance and alignment (Lesson 2).
- [SnowProfessor — "First Snowboard Lesson" (8-video beginner series)](https://snowprofessor.com/step-1-first-snowboard-lesson)
  Clear beginner terminology and a defined milestone progression. Use for: vocabulary and checking sequencing.
- [Burton — "Learn to Snowboard: Quick and Easy Tips for Beginners"](https://www.burton.com/en-us/blogs/the-burton-blog/learn-to-snowboard)
  Brand, but written with certified instructors. Use for: how to fall safely, glide & skate drill, falling leaf → C-turn sequence.

### Progression & expectation-setting

- [The Snow Centre — "How to Learn to Snowboard"](https://thesnowcentre.com/blog/how-to-learn-to-snowboard-a-complete-guide-for-beginner/)
  A real indoor centre's Stage 1–4 pathway and the "~6 lessons to feel comfortable" benchmark. Use for: calibrating realistic timelines.
- [The Snow Centre — dry slope vs real snow](https://thesnowcentre.com/blog/learning-to-snowboard-on-a-dry-slope-vs-real-snow-what-is-the-difference/)
  Surface differences and what to wear indoors. Use for: dome-specific expectations.
- [WikiHow — "How to Snowboard" (with instructor Kent Bry, fact-checked)](https://www.wikihow.com/Snowboard)
  Secondary, but the "unlearning a bad habit takes ~10× longer than learning it right" line is a useful motivator. Use for: garlands-before-turns argument.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/snowboarding](https://reddit.com/r/snowboarding)
  Large, active subreddit with a beginner wiki and gear/boot-fitting megathreads. Use for: gear questions, "is this normal?", boot-fit sanity checks.
- [SnowboardForum](https://www.snowboardforum.com/)
  Long-running forum; more technical/technique discussion than Reddit. Use for: technique debates, movement-analysis questions.
- Local: **the snowdome itself.** Even self-directed, a single drop-in group or private lesson mid-course gives external eyes on your form — the one thing a lesson file can't do. Strongly consider one around Lesson 7.

## Gaps

- No single free, high-trust resource found for **movement analysis of your own riding from video**. If this becomes relevant, search "AASI movement analysis beginner" closer to the trip.
- No dome-specific drill library (indoor slopes are short with limited pitch); current drills are adapted from mountain resources and may need shortening.
