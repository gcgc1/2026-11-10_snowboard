# Prior experience: a few days riding, sideslipping only

The learner has ridden a handful of days and can sideslip / skid down a slope, but cannot yet link clean turns. This sets the floor for the course: first-contact basics (gear fit, strapping in, standing up, deciding lead foot) can be skipped, and the course can open directly on the turn model and build toward linking turns on a green run.

**Evidence**: stated directly during mission-setting ("a few days here and there", turns described as messy).

**Implications**:
- Start at Lesson 1 (the turn model), not at gear/first-steps.
- Edging drills (Lesson 3) will be partly review — position them as *refinement* of an existing sideslip, not first contact.
- The likely active misconception is **counter-rotation** (swinging the back foot to turn); predict it will surface and target it in Lesson 5.
