# Mission: Snowboarding Fundamentals

## Why

A snowboarding trip is 1–3 months away, and the goal is to **keep up with friends and family** on the mountain — riding the same runs as the group instead of trailing behind, stopping constantly, or sitting one out. The four hours of lessons exist to turn a few messy days on a board into real, controlled riding by the time the trip arrives.

## Success looks like

- Link turns down a green run *in control* — turning both ways, dictating my own speed, not just surviving.
- Ride the button lift and chairlift, and get on and off, without drama.
- Follow the group down beginner and easy intermediate runs **at their pace**, not stop-start.
- Finish a run relaxed and in control — not frightened, not exhausted.

## Constraints

- **~4 hours of lesson time in total.** Every lesson has to earn its place.
- **1–3 months of runway**, so practice can be spaced out for retention rather than crammed.
- Practice happens at an **indoor snowdome**, **self-directed** (no coach).
- Background: ridden a few days; can sideslip / skid down a slope, but turns are messy.
- The bottleneck is **turning** — the whole course builds toward linking turns.

## Out of scope

- Freestyle / park, jumps, rails, butters.
- Carving at speed, steeps, off-piste, powder.
- Switch riding.
- Deep gear-buying research (renting / resort demo is plenty for now).
